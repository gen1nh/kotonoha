## Direction contract

THESIS: Kotonoha’s primary study loop is one clear question, one answer, a short explanation, and a concrete next step. The app keeps the lesson and progress visible without presenting itself as a generic dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned editorial Japanese study. Light paper (#F3F0E8), dark ink (#1D1E1C), restrained red (#A83B32), quiet paper panels (#E9E5DB), strong system typography with Japanese fallbacks, fine dividers, and explicit text labels. No illustrative decoration. Red marks selection or the current step, never meaning alone.

STORY: Arrive at today’s study; read one Japanese prompt; answer by touch or keyboard; receive a concise explanation; continue, review, or take a paper exercise. Progress stays local and each section remains reachable through conventional app navigation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Match the chosen question-first sketch: familiar top navigation; a compact left column states level, objective, and session progress; the wider work area shows the Japanese question, its short instruction, and clearly labeled answer choices; explanation and next-question action appear after selection. On mobile, stack context above the question and preserve large touch targets. Home leads with the next actionable question; Learn, Review, Paper, JLPT, and Settings retain distinct routes.

FORM: User-selected “Uma pergunta por vez” from the safer hand; seed key ba007431. Keep question, answer, explanation, completion, and continue as an accessible interaction. Reach this form across all N5–N1 lessons without forcing the handwriting canvas, practice-on-paper workflow, settings, or JLPT log into a quiz. Risk: familiar interaction, so Japanese examples and helpful explanations must carry the product’s specificity.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
