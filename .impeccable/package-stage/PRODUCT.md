# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A person studying Japanese independently, from beginner level through JLPT N5–N1, on a phone or computer.

## Product Purpose

Kotonoha helps one learner build and maintain Japanese study habits through lessons, practice, review, and progress tracking. Success means that the learner can study consistently across the JLPT levels while keeping their progress on their own device.

## Positioning

Kotonoha is a personal, local-first study notebook that combines interactive Japanese practice with exercises intended for paper and a learner-controlled progress record.

## Operating Context

The learner uses the app on mobile or desktop, often in short daily sessions. They may switch between browser practice and writing answers by hand. A deployed HTTPS build can be installed and used offline; external services require connectivity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app includes kana charts with browser speech, vocabulary and spaced review, grammar notes and quiz, a handwriting canvas, daily paper exercises, a question-first daily practice, JLPT level plans, mock-test logging, optional Groq-generated suggestions, and JSON export/import. N4, N3, N2, and N1 each have three original interactive lessons of four questions and 16 vocabulary cards. Study data is stored in the browser on the learner's device. The Groq key is session-only. Japanese voice availability varies by device. The handwriting canvas does not evaluate handwriting or stroke order. The JLPT does not publish exhaustive official vocabulary, kanji, and grammar lists; Kotonoha's level-organized lessons are a didactic reference, not an official syllabus or exam guarantee. JLPT links point to official practice materials and do not reproduce protected test papers.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Kotonoha. The user selected an editorial Japanese-study direction: light paper, dark ink, restrained red accent, strong typography, and no illustrations. Preserve existing product functionality and identity assets.

## Evidence on Hand

The working React/Vite app and its source data are in this project. Existing local content includes 46 hiragana, 46 katakana, 20 N5 vocabulary entries, grammar notes, and JLPT plans. No claim of official JLPT affiliation or complete exam coverage is established; do not imply either.

## Product Principles

- Keep study progress under the learner's control on their device.
- Support practical, repeatable study sessions across mobile and desktop.
- Pair interactive exercises with deliberate handwriting and paper practice.
- Be clear about the difference between study guidance and official JLPT materials.
